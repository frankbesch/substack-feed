Where to Draw the Line on AI Autonomy

Public acceptance versus worker desire, on one map

AI adoption is not just about technology. It's about understanding the delicate balance between public moral acceptance and worker desire.

This AI Deployment Map is a reminder of where to draw the line between autonomy, co-pilot assistance, and purely assistive functions.
