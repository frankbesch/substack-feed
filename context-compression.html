If Your Prompt Is Getting Longer, You Might Be Getting Worse

A short take on context, compression, and starting from the decision

2026-04-18

An AI read my USPS tracking number and confidently reported it as a nine-sextillion-digit number.

I saw a package.

Context matters. And the way most teams add context is making things worse.

Stop shipping AI-generated context without compression. A recent study of repository-level context files for coding agents (Gloaguen et al., arXiv:2602.11988) found the pattern: more tokens bring more constraints, and more constraints bring worse performance.

The trap is subtle. AI-generated context looks high quality. It is clean, structured, and comprehensive. But most of it is repackaged information the model already knows. No new signal, just noise with confidence.

High-performing teams ask a different question. Not "what else should we add?" but "what can we remove?"

Optimizing AI for cost is the same distraction from the other side. Cheap prompts that drive weak decisions are expensive. In sales and presales, that shows up as slower deals and lost revenue.

So start with the decision, not the task.

  • Include only the context that changes the answer.
  • Force a tight output: verdict, then drivers, then risks.

Fewer prompts. Better decisions. Faster outcomes.

Efficiency is tactical. Efficacy is leverage.
